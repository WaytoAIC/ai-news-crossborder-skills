# Cross-Border Ecommerce Scoring

Use this rubric after collecting AI HOT items. Score strictly: a hot AI update is not automatically useful to sellers.

## Score Levels

| Score | Meaning | Include? |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Directly changes a seller workflow this week: creative testing, listing, ads, research, compliance, automation, or customer path. | Yes |
| 4 | Strong near-term opportunity, but needs one validation step before adoption. | Yes |
| 3 | Useful signal for operators or content, but action is indirect. | Optional |
| 2 | Interesting AI news with weak ecommerce path. | Usually no |
| 1 | No practical seller value now. | No |

## Positive Signals

- Reduces cost or cycle time for main images, A+, videos, UGC scripts, localization, or ad variations.
- Improves keyword/listing/search visibility, buyer intent capture, or AI shopping discovery.
- Helps analyze ads, SQP, reviews, VOC, competitors, trends, or category opportunities.
- Changes compliance risk for claims, fake reviews, AI-generated endorsements, copyright, privacy, or platform policy.
- Improves team execution through Codex, Claude Code, MCP, agents, spreadsheets, Feishu, or recurring reports.
- Changes how buyers search, compare, ask agents, or discover products.
- Exposes security or account risk in seller automation workflows.

## Negative Signals

- Pure benchmark/model-ranking news with no workflow consequence.
- Funding/IPO/personnel news unless it affects seller tools, platform access, or cost.
- Research papers with no near-term application path.
- Tool launch without evidence of production usability, rights safety, export quality, or workflow fit.
- Hype around "agents" with no clear seller task.

## Required Fields For Included Items

For each included item, produce:

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 价值等级 | High / Medium / Watch / Exclude |
| 跨境影响 | One concrete mechanism, not generic optimism |
| 可执行动作 | A test, SOP change, content topic, or risk check |
| 适合卖家 | Boutique seller, content team, ads operator, sourcing team, agency, etc. |
| 风险 | What not to assume or where to verify |
| 原始来源 | Original source title/name and URL |

## Default Ranking Tie-Breakers

1. Direct seller action beats general strategic insight.
2. Compliance/security risks outrank nice-to-have tools.
3. Workflow tools outrank model launches unless the model launch changes cost or capability.
4. Content-worthy items need a clear seller lesson, not just a hot headline.
